How to read a
before and after.

Lighting does more work than most treatments. A photograph taken under a window will always flatter against one taken under a ceiling light, and a clinic that changes the angle between the two shots is telling you something about the result.

Ask three questions of any gallery, including this one. How many sessions produced that? How long between the two photographs? And what skin type is the client, because a result on Fitzpatrick II tells you very little about what will happen on Fitzpatrick V.

Every client shown here gave written permission. Where a face is not shown, that is the client's choice rather than a crop hiding something.
