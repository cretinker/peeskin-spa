Our Origin & Philosophy

Why this clinic
exists.

For decades, Nigerian women and men have been told that advanced aesthetic treatments were not safe for them. "Laser will burn your skin." "Peels will leave permanent patches." "Injectables are not for African facial structures."

Almost every burn story in Nigeria traces back to the same preventable error: clinics operating imported devices calibrated solely for Caucasian skin, led by practitioners with zero specialized training in melanin-rich Fitzpatrick IV to VI skin biology.

At Pee Skin & MedSpa, we built the entire facility around the scientific reality of Black skin. That means true 1064nm Nd:YAG lasers that bypass epidermal pigment safely, medical peels formulated without rebound pigmentation risk, strictly published upfront prices, and the clinical courage to say "no" when a procedure will not serve a client's health.
