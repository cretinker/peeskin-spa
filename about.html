Why this
place exists.

Nigerian women have spent years being told that certain treatments are not for them. Laser will burn you. Peels will leave you patchy. Filler is for other faces. Most of that advice came from clinics using equipment designed for pale skin, and from practitioners who had never been trained on anything else.

None of it is true with the right device and the right settings. What is true is that the margin for error is smaller, that a patch test matters more, and that a practitioner who has read the research on Fitzpatrick IV to VI will get a different result from one who has not.

So we built the clinic around that. One practitioner who trains continuously, equipment chosen for the skin actually walking through the door, published prices so nobody is negotiated with, and a willingness to say no when a treatment will not do what a client hopes.
