The one rule: nothing new inside six weeks.

Not a first peel, not a first laser session, not a filler you have been thinking about, not a facial from somewhere you have never been. Skin reacts unpredictably the first time it meets a treatment, and a reaction six weeks out is a story you laugh about later. A reaction six days out is not.
