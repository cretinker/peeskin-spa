The Clinical Reality

Preserving African facial anatomy

Melanin-rich skin retains dermal thickness and elasticity longer than Caucasian skin, but volume loss in the mid-face fat pads and dynamic expression lines (frown lines, forehead creases) still develop over time.

African facial architecture features distinct cheekbone projection, fuller lips, and robust structural support. Applying standard Western injection templates risks creating an unnatural, "pillow-face" distortion.

Our doctor-led approach emphasizes micro-aliquots: conservative placement of hyaluronic acid gel and targeted botulinum toxin that softens fatigue lines while preserving full emotional facial movement.
